# Catalog and Image Assets

## Product Catalog

`catalogue.json` contains 18 products, 36 variants, and 6 categories covering reusable household goods, personal care products, bags, and drinkware.

The Prisma seed and EcoBot read the same catalog. The seed also creates development accounts, orders, and reviews. Product names, prices, stock levels, customer details, and environmental descriptions are illustrative data rather than commercial or certification claims.

Rerunning the seed preserves existing catalog records, account passwords, and inventory changes.

## Illustrations

The product illustrations, hero image, and placeholder are SVG assets generated by:

```sh
node scripts/generate-demo-assets.mjs
```

Assets are written to `frontend/public/images`. Their paths are stored in the catalog so the storefront and chatbot can display the same product images.

## Existing Uploads

Earlier JPEG uploads are archived in `.local/legacy-uploads` and excluded from source distributions. They are not used by the seed; their original usage rights have not been established.

The interface loads fonts and Material Symbols from Google Fonts. Product and hero illustrations are included in the repository.
